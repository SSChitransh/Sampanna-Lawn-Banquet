What types of events can I enquire about?

You can enquire about weddings, receptions, engagements, family celebrations, birthdays, anniversaries and suitable corporate gatherings.

How do I get prices and package details?

Pricing can depend on the event date, guest count, venue space and requirements. Contact the venue directly for a current quote and package inclusions.

How can I check availability?

Send your preferred event date and approximate guest count through the WhatsApp enquiry form. The venue team can confirm availability directly.

Can I ask about decor, catering or accommodation?

Yes. Add your requirements to the enquiry and ask the team to confirm which services and arrangements are available for your chosen date.
